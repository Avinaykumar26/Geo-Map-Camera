Where do my photos go?

Nowhere. Everything — camera frames, GPS fixes, map tiles and the final image — is handled by JavaScript running on your device. Saving writes a file to your own storage.

Which network requests does the app make?

Two, both to OpenStreetMap: raster map tiles for the mini-map, and an optional reverse-geocode lookup that turns your coordinates into a street address. Neither carries your photo.

Why does it need HTTPS?

Browsers only hand out camera and GPS access on secure origins. That is a browser rule, not an account requirement.
